Know your Odoo screens

A tour of the Odoo apps used at FEGIS. Each screenshot is numbered; the list under it explains what each part is for.

Start with "The basics": lists, forms, buttons and the status bar work the same way in every app. Click a screenshot to enlarge it.

1The basics

Every Odoo app follows the same layout: a list of records, a form for one record, buttons that move it forward, and a history panel. Learn these three screens and you can find your way around any app.

2Requisitions

Internal requests for items, from office supplies to site materials. A requisition passes through two approvals, department then IR, before items are bought from a supplier or taken from stock.

3Purchase

Requests for quotation (RFQs) and purchase orders sent to suppliers. An RFQ becomes a purchase order once it is approved, and only then is it sent to the vendor.

4Approvals

Requests that need sign-off but don't belong to another app: payment applications, contracts, business trips, borrowed items, petty cash, car rental and casual recruitment. Each type has its own list of required approvers.

5Expenses

Money staff spend for work and want back, such as transport tickets. Expenses are grouped into a report, approved, posted to accounting, then paid.

6Accounting

Customer invoices, supplier bills, bank and mobile money accounts, and financial reports. Most staff only read here; Finance does the posting and payments.

7Inventory

Stock held at each FEGIS warehouse (Kigali Main, Kanombe, Bugesera) and every movement of goods in, out, or between them.

8Attendances

Check-in and check-out records for every employee, captured at the kiosk. These records are the basis for hours worked, extra hours and payroll.

9Time Off

Leave requests and leave balances. An employee asks for days off, a manager approves, and in some cases a second approver validates before the leave is final.

10Sign

Electronic signatures. Upload a PDF, mark where people must sign, and send it. Odoo keeps the signed copy and a record of who signed and when.

11Words you will see

Odoo terms used throughout this guide, in plain words.

TermMeaning
RecordOne item in Odoo: one requisition, one invoice, one expense.
Stage / statusWhere a record is in its process, e.g. Draft, Submitted, Approved, Done.
DraftNot final yet. It can still be changed and nobody else acts on it.
PostedFinal and recorded in the accounts. It can only be reversed, not edited.
RFQRequest for quotation: asking a supplier for a price before ordering.
Purchase order (PO)An approved order to buy from a supplier, numbered like P00130.
Requisition (IR)Internal requisition: a staff request for items, numbered like IR00193.
VendorA supplier FEGIS buys from.
Analytic accountThe project, site or department a cost is charged to.
JournalA group of accounting entries, e.g. one bank account, sales or purchases.
ReconcileMatch bank statement lines with entries in the books so both agree.
Receipt (Inventory)Goods arriving at a warehouse.
Transfer / pickingAny movement of goods into, out of, or between stores.
ActivityA task assigned to a person with a due date, shown under the clock icon.
ChatterThe history and message panel on every record.
AllocationThe number of leave days an employee is given for a type of time off.
ValidateThe final approval of a time off request.
Sample dataFaded example rows Odoo shows on an empty screen. They are not real records.
Unit of measureHow a quantity is counted: Pcs, BOX, Unit, kg, litre.
